Common questions about 3D Scan Pro.

Frequently asked questions

Which iPhone or iPad models do I need?

Walk Around and Turntable use the LiDAR scanner, so they need an iPhone or iPad with LiDAR (Pro models). 360 Spin needs no LiDAR and works on any supported iPhone. Object Capture and Macro have their own camera requirements, and the App Store listing shows which devices are compatible.

What file formats can I export?

The free tier exports OBJ (with vertex colors) and USDZ (Apple's AR format), up to 3 times per week. Pro Unlock adds binary STL export with millimeter scaling for 3D printing slicers and removes the weekly export limit.

What does Fix for 3D Printing do?

It runs an automated repair pipeline that removes degenerate and duplicate triangles, welds close vertices, fills holes, fixes normals, and, when needed, runs a Marching Cubes voxel remesh to produce a closed, watertight mesh for your slicer.

Is Pro Unlock a subscription?

No. Pro Unlock is a one-time $9.99 purchase with no recurring monthly or annual fees.

How do I restore my Pro Unlock purchase on another device?

Your purchase is linked to your Apple Account. Open the paywall or settings screen in 3D Scan Pro while signed in to the same Apple Account and tap Restore Purchases.

Are my 3D scans uploaded to the cloud?

No. All LiDAR capture, photogrammetry reconstruction, mesh repair, and file exporting run locally on your iPhone or iPad. Your scans never leave your device unless you share or export them.
