Privacy policy

3D Scan Pro, published by Emrld Labs.

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Summary

3D Scan Pro performs all 3D scanning, photogrammetry, mesh repair, and file exports locally on your device. Emrld Labs collects no personal data, uses no analytics or advertising frameworks, and never uploads your camera feed or 3D models to a server.

App Store privacy label: Data Not Collected. The developer does not collect any data from this app.

What we collect

None. The App Store privacy label is Data Not Collected.

What stays on your device

  • Saved 3D scan projects, mesh geometry, textures, and thumbnails in the app's local Documents directory
  • App preferences and weekly free export count stored in local UserDefaults
  • Pro Unlock purchase status managed by Apple StoreKit

Permissions

  • Camera and LiDAR Sensor: Used in real time on your device to capture 3D mesh geometry and surface colors during scanning. Camera data is never transmitted off your device.

You can change these permissions at any time in Settings.

Network use

The app contains no URLSession networking code and performs no cloud processing. Network access is used only by Apple StoreKit when purchasing or restoring Pro Unlock.

Purchases

Pro Unlock is a one-time in-app purchase processed by Apple through the App Store. Emrld Labs never receives or stores your payment information.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
